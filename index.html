02
App Overview

What the App Does

Cricket Scorer Pro is a mobile cricket scoring application. Its features include:

  • Recording ball-by-ball cricket scores for matches and tournaments
  • Storing player names, team names, and match statistics locally
  • Generating batting and bowling scorecards per innings
  • Managing Round Robin and Knockout tournaments with standings tables
  • Offline-first operation — no internet connection required at any point

All data is entered manually by the user and stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on the user's own device.

04
Local Storage

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores data locally using SQLite — a standard embedded database. This data is created entirely by you and remains under your control at all times.

What is stored
  • Team names and player names that you enter manually
  • Match scores, ball-by-ball records, and innings statistics
  • Tournament names, fixtures, and standings
  • App preferences (e.g. over count settings)
Where it lives

All data is written to the app's private sandboxed storage directory on your device (/data/data/com.example.cricket_scorer/ on Android). No other app can access this directory.

How to delete your data
  • Delete individual matches from the Home screen (swipe left → Delete)
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data permanently
  • Device Settings → Apps → Cricket Scorer Pro → Clear Data

Integrations

Third-Party Services

Cricket Scorer Pro does not integrate with, connect to, or share data with any third-party service. Specifically:

  • No analytics SDK — Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc. are not included
  • No advertising SDK — AdMob, Facebook Ads, ironSource are not included
  • No crash-reporting SDK — Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag are not included
  • No social login — Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, Facebook Login are not used
  • No cloud backup — iCloud sync, Google Drive, Firebase are not used

The only external code used is the Flutter framework and standard open-source packages (sqflite, uuid, intl, path_provider). None of these libraries collect, transmit, or process personal data.

08
Safety

Data Security

Because all data is stored locally and never transmitted, the security of your cricket data depends on the security of your device. We recommend:

  • Using a screen lock or biometric authentication on your device
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Not sharing your device with untrusted individuals

We do not have access to your data and therefore cannot "breach" it on our end. Your data is as secure as your phone.

User Rights

Your Rights

Since we do not collect or hold any personal data on our servers, data-subject rights are exercised directly on your device. You have:

  • Right to access — Open the app at any time to view all stored matches and tournaments.
  • Right to delete — Delete any match, tournament, or all data by clearing app storage.
  • Right to portability — Your SQLite database is stored in your device's app folder.

If you are located in the EU/EEA, UK, or another jurisdiction with data protection laws, you may contact us with any privacy concerns and we will respond within 30 days.
